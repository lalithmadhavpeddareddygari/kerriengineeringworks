Products

Pneumatic sample conveying, macro etching and dust conveying—designed and built in-house for your plant layout.

Horizontal system

Cream C-loop receiving station. Very robust mechanics for long transfer distances and demanding plant environments.

Propulsion from a dedicated blower or from plant compressed air when pressure and quality are suitable for the route.

  • Preferred where distance and duty cycle favour a horizontal receiving loop
  • Routing engineered for bends, vertical rises and multiple send points
  • Transport speeds exceeding 100 km/h on suitable runs
  • Results returned to operation areas within seconds

Vertical system

Blue upright straight-post station with ring blower and plastic capsules.

Best for short distances, congested laboratories, or plants without reliable compressed air. The same capsule transport principles apply; blower or plant air can be used depending on site utilities.

  • Compact footprint for space-limited labs
  • Ring blower package when plant air is unavailable
  • Custom tube routing for congested plant areas
  • Custom-designed for each facility's layout and needs

Which system, and how it is designed

Choice between horizontal and vertical receiving stations depends on distance, laboratory space, routing complexity and air supply—not on sample mass. We do not rate systems by sample weight.

Factor Horizontal (C-loop) Vertical (upright)
Typical distance Long runs, multi-point plants Short runs, congested routes
Receiving station Cream C-loop, high mechanical robustness Blue straight-post with ring blower
Air supply Blower or plant compressed air Ring blower and/or plant compressed air
Lab space Spacious or standard layouts Tight or crowded laboratory areas

Site-specific design

Every quotation starts from your layout: congested or spacious lab, short or long distance, complex routing, with or without plant compressed air.

Macro etching units

Macro etching units for examining steel macrostructure in plant and centralised laboratories. Built for heavy industrial environments and integration alongside pneumatic sample transport.

  • Designed for continuous operation in steel plants
  • Supplied with installation and commissioning support

Dust conveying systems

Pneumatic dust conveying for steel plants and similar environments. Layout and collector interface are engineered to match duct paths, material load and plant utilities.

  • Industrial dust transport in steel and heavy industry
  • Customised routing and blower duty for the facility
  • Manufactured and commissioned in-house
