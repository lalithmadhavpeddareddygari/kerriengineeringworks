About Kerri Engineering Works

Special-purpose machinery for pneumatic sample transport and plant laboratory equipment, from design through commissioning.

Company

Who we are

Kerri Engineering Works is based in Autonagar, Visakhapatnam. We serve steel, cement, foundry and aluminium plants in India and international markets.

Our pneumatic sample conveying systems move material from metallurgical processing areas to centralised laboratories for chemical analysis. Transport speeds exceed 100 km/h on suitable routes; results return to operation areas within seconds.

We design, manufacture, supply, install and commission conveying systems and macro etching units for plants that require reliable, rapid sample logistics.

In-house capability

What we do

Mechanical design, fabrication, assembly, site installation and commissioning are handled by our Visakhapatnam works and field team. Systems are built around horizontal C-loop or vertical upright receiving stations, with routing and blower duty matched to each plant.

Macro etching units and dust conveying lines follow the same project path: site survey, detailed design, manufacture, delivery, installation and performance testing.
