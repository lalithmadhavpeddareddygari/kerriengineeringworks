Our Story

Kerri Engineering Works is a trusted provider of innovative pneumatic conveying solutions tailored to meet the specific needs of heavy industries such as steel, cement, and aluminum.

With over two decades of experience, we have built a reputation for delivering high-quality pneumatic sample conveying systems and laboratory equipment that enhance efficiency and streamline sample testing processes — from metallurgical processing areas to centralized laboratories for chemical analysis — and transfer results back to operation areas within seconds.

We design, manufacture, supply, install, and commission pneumatic sample conveying systems and macro etching systems that redefine operational efficiency.
