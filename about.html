About Kerri Engineering Works

Designing, manufacturing, installing and commissioning pneumatic sample transport and plant laboratory equipment since 2000.

Who we are

Kerri Engineering Works is based in Autonagar, Visakhapatnam. We serve steel, cement, foundry and aluminium plants in India and international markets.

Our pneumatic sample conveying systems move material from metallurgical processing areas to centralised laboratories for chemical analysis. Transport speeds exceed 100 km/h on suitable routes; results return to operation areas within seconds.

We design, manufacture, supply, install and commission conveying systems and macro etching units for plants that require reliable, rapid sample logistics.
