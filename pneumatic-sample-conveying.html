Pneumatic sample conveying system

One product line with two receiving-station solutions—horizontal C-loop or vertical upright—engineered from your plant layout and laboratory footprint.

Rapid sample logistics

Pneumatic sample conveying moves material from metallurgical processing areas to centralised laboratories for chemical analysis.

Transport speeds exceed 100 km/h on suitable routes; results return to operation areas within seconds.

Two solutions

Both configurations share the same pneumatic product scope; the receiving station and routing differ by site.

Horizontal C-loop solution

Cream receiving loop for long runs, demanding duty and blower or plant air.

Vertical upright solution

Compact blue straight-post station with ring blower for tight laboratories.

Horizontal vs vertical receiving station

Choice depends on distance, laboratory space, routing complexity and air supply—not on sample mass. We do not rate systems by sample weight.

Factor Horizontal (C-loop) Vertical (upright)
Typical distance Long runs, multi-point plants Short runs, congested routes
Receiving station Cream C-loop, high mechanical robustness Blue straight-post with ring blower
Air supply Blower or plant compressed air Ring blower and/or plant compressed air
Lab space Spacious or standard layouts Tight or crowded laboratory areas

Site-specific design

Every quotation starts from your layout: congested or spacious lab, short or long distance, complex routing, with or without plant compressed air.
