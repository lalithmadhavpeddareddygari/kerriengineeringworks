Robust mechanics for long transfer distances. Propulsion from a dedicated blower or plant compressed air when suitable.

When plants choose horizontal

Preferred where distance and duty cycle favour a horizontal receiving loop. Routing is engineered for bends, vertical rises and multiple send points from melt shops and casting areas to centralised laboratories.

  • Transport speeds exceeding 100 km/h on suitable runs
  • Results returned to operation areas within seconds
  • Steel, cement, foundry and aluminium installations
  • Site-specific design—congested or spacious lab, with or without plant air

Manufacturing and components

Stations, pipe bends and wear sections are fabricated and assembled at our Autonagar works with Class 1 Make in India local content (over 80%).

  • Air preparation and regulators matched to plant duty
